# TACITUS: focus, product direction and twenty next actions

Reviewed 12 November 2026. Public strategy; private repositories and source/release details are excluded. Historical-purpose notices have been merged for public retirement candidates. Archives remain pending administration access and, where applicable, dependency preservation; no repository was permanently deleted.

TACITUS is building a policy research workspace: use saved sources, institutional procedure and organisational context to produce a useful reviewed brief, then update it as evidence changes. The invited [App](https://app.tacitus.me/) and the [public reference pipeline](https://github.com/sargonxg/TACITUS-Knowledge-Pipeline-open) have different release and data boundaries. PRAXIS remains a separate paused workspace. Broad specialist coordination, customer efficacy and a contributor marketplace remain development goals.

The approved App direction serves small teams in corporate policy, conflict analysis and US/EU legislative analysis. Work is private by default, uses curated packs and bounded on-demand research, and remains subject to the App owner’s release gates. Invited testers are free; commercial offers are quote-based seats plus measured usage. The $100/month planning allowance is not an enforced infrastructure invoice cap. Keep one product engine and a small set of supporting contracts. Native research, historical source kits and upstream evaluations should earn further investment through a named consumer or falsifiable task.

| 1 | NOW | **Choose a recurring customer problem** — Prepare recurring tasks in all three approved domains; recruit suitable analysts and two qualified reviewers per domain. | Three analysts complete a real recurring task twice and identify a useful output and willingness-to-pay condition. |
| 2 | NOW | **Reconcile the canonical App release** — Review the App and its graph, framework and evaluator dependencies in order; distinguish main, candidate, executable and deployed versions. | One current release record identifies exact source/artifact/migration hashes and acceptance limits; main accurately explains the release. |
| 3 | NOW | **Finish one source-to-updated-brief journey** — Use the existing App, source library, capsules and graph: save evidence, confirm context, draft, review, export, then propose an update. | A new analyst completes a supplied permissioned task in each approved domain without developer assistance; the approved original and evidence remain inspectable. |
| 4 | NOW | **Close credential and ownership gaps** — Verify credential hygiene, current App account/profile isolation and the release’s live acceptance. | Provider revocation/replacement receipts and two-account plus private/practice-profile live acceptance; no credential value in source or logs. |
| 5 | NEXT | **Complete document intake** — Connect the existing isolated parser and bounded original storage to the App rather than adding another ingestion application. | A permitted real PDF retains its original hash, edition, rights and exact extracted passages; unsupported/failed extraction is visible and recoverable. |
| 6 | NEXT | **Make organisational context editable and scoped** — Version team responsibilities, terminology, analytical methods and user-declared preferences; separate personal, project and shared context. | Users inspect, correct and revoke context; changes require explicit selection/review and never leak across accounts or infer sensitive preferences. |
| 7 | NEXT | **Ship excellent procedural knowledge packs** — Author narrow jurisdiction/date-specific legislative and institutional-process packs with expert review, exact primary sources and reuse rights. | Each pack distinguishes proposal, adoption, entry into force and applicability; a qualified reviewer approves a worked task and limitations. |
| 8 | NEXT | **Integrate reviewed graph layers** — Use the existing graph implementation for distinct entities, exact mentions, versioned ontologies and reviewed semantic relationships. | Namesakes remain separate; proposed links cannot silently become accepted context; historical packet readers remain compatible. |
| 9 | NEXT | **Select context deliberately** — Explain why a passage or framework was selected, record omissions, and keep instructions separate from quoted evidence. | A bounded task retains decisive exceptions/negation and uses only authorized source versions; relevance is assessed against a matched simple baseline. |
| 10 | NEXT | **Make specialist execution resumable** — Use the App workflow plan and durable execution ledger for researcher, procedure checker, writer and reviewer stages. | Close/reopen/cancel and uncertain dispatch do not duplicate model calls or charges; recorded activity is distinct from hidden model reasoning. |
| 11 | NEXT | **Activate bounded official-source research only after acceptance** — Complete the existing search/fetch adapters, private-original receipts, request consent, deadlines and shared allowance before enabling production capabilities. | Private-network/redirect/prompt-injection cases are refused; snippets remain discovery; failed work and full provider costs are retained. |
| 12 | NEXT | **Track freshness and propagate corrections** — Bind claims and artifacts to source editions; propose changes when a source is corrected, withdrawn or superseded. | An updated source marks affected conclusions for re-review, preserves the earlier brief and never equates retrieval time with legal currency. |
| 13 | NEXT | **Agree format contracts before adapters** — Use the published reference/native compatibility matrix; let the App owner define project-scoped versions, including Unicode code points, UTF-8 bytes and reader-specific UTF-16 boundaries, rights, omissions and review state. | An independent reader rejects tampering, unsupported formats and lossy transfers; a complete round trip retains exact quotations and source identities. |
| 14 | NEXT | **Run the prepared held-out review** — Acquire 50 permissioned held-out tasks per domain and two qualified reviewers per domain; use the canonical evaluator with frozen matched tasks, same models/evidence, blinded expert ratings and retained failures. | Report supported claims, salience, qualifier preservation, editing time, latency and total cost; do not market synthetic test counts as efficacy. |
| 15 | NEXT | **Prove first-use and mobile usability** — Observe actual analysts using the simple desk, context picker, Knowledge view and exports; retain keyboard/reduced-motion/mobile support. | First useful brief takes a documented number of steps; users can recover from mistakes at 320/390/768/1440px without developer instructions. |
| 16 | NEXT | **Exercise recovery and data lifecycle** — Test backup/restore, interrupted writes, source access, private exports, retention and account/content deletion without erasing audit meaning. | A fixture workspace is restored with verified hashes, rights, profile isolation and historical reviews; user-facing retention/deletion rules match behavior. |
| 17 | LATER | **Price measured work sustainably** — Measure model/search/storage/support costs per useful deliverable before enabling paid usage or broad automatic model routing. | A transparent allowance and contribution-margin model uses actual attempts, failures and support effort; checkout stays disabled until its separate acceptance. |
| 18 | LATER | **Build an expert contribution system before a marketplace** — Define pack ownership, licenses, review standards, versions, revocation and private sharing before transactional distribution. | An expert can publish a reviewed licensed pack with a reproducible example; permissions and withdrawal propagate correctly; no premature marketplace promise. |
| 19 | NOW | **Reduce competing implementations** — Use the 87-repository lifecycle proposal: extract useful patterns, archive redundant prototypes, retain bounded research and replace maintained forks with pinned upstream references. | Every active repository has an owner role and consumer/task; unique history/rights and deployment references survive; personal projects remain independent. |
| 20 | NOW | **Make release contracts and public claims continuously checkable** — Run the new offline App vendor-integrity gate, exact consumer tests and release receipts; route both GitHub profiles to a small set of useful examples. | Dependency drift and stale/missing receipts fail CI; public navigation, concept-art captions, credits and release stages stay accurate without exposing private source. |

## Current independent verification

The public reference pipeline and organization starter each pass their 42-test suite and actual historical example build. Workbench passes 113 configured browser/reader tests with zero skips, 14 importer tests and inventory checks, using its unchanged pinned native helpers. See its [format/rights/review matrix](https://github.com/sargonxg/tacitus-workbench/blob/main/docs/FORMAT_COMPATIBILITY.md) and [local checkpoint](https://github.com/sargonxg/tacitus-workbench/blob/main/docs/LOCAL_VERIFICATION_2026-11-12.md). These are local evidence and recorded examples, not hosted App acceptance or demonstrated customer benefit.

DIALECTICA v3 passes 81 Rust tests (one explicit integration skip), five Python tests and its fixture gates; the optional native Ladybug fixture returns nine nodes with the matching 0.17.1 library. AGON passes 75 local tests; dependency-security scope is recorded in its local checkpoint and remains a separate release gate. KAIROS passes nine tests with strict Clippy. These experimental engines retain distinct contracts and are not newly adopted App dependencies.

Retirement is archival only: preserve source/attribution, test restoration, clear consumers/deployments, then archive with appropriate administration access. Existing archives remain preserved. LEANN additionally needs its external submodule/LFS assets preserved. PRAXIS and Poliora retain existing users, security maintenance and holds; neither receives a competing product expansion.

The [study protocol](VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md) is prepared. No participant study has run and no customer benefit is claimed. The App owner controls runtime, packs, permissions, migrations, release reconciliation and deployment. The personal-site owner controls personal sites, poetry, music, photography and personal artwork.
